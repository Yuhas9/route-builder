Commercial Front Loader

Separate commercial route collection for commercial dumpsters and restaurant dumpsters. Stops in this mode are stored separately from residential routes. Front-loader routing does not apply the residential right-side rule.

Commercial mode keeps its own route and stop records. Confirm truck access, backing, clearance, and service instructions before driving. Road optimization is not yet a verified front-loader routing engine.

Map layers
Zoom past level 18 to enlarge the last available satellite tiles rather than display blank squares. New street names and addresses still depend on the provider's data. In local HTML previews, OpenStreetMap road tiles are blocked by its server policy; this app automatically uses Esri Street instead. OpenStreetMap roads can be selected when Route Builder is hosted on HTTPS (such as GitHub Pages). If those tiles fail, Esri Street loads automatically.
House numbers — Arkansas GIS
Your own entered/imported customer addresses appear beside green pins at zoom 18 or closer using the address-label toggle. Pins shrink when zoomed out. Arkansas GIS labels are a separate optional layer.
House numbers turn on automatically at zoom 17+. Click a number to add its customer pin. You can also toggle House Numbers on the map. The GPX scanner is separate and unchanged. Customer address labels are placed opposite the house side of the nearest GPX road; use Label to adjust.
Uses Arkansas public address-point records, not Google Maps. Coverage may be incomplete, especially in new developments. This is an online layer; no bulk address download is included.

Compare the map area with Arkansas GIS, Benton County addresses, and available OpenStreetMap address tags. New addresses are saved for review; they do not become customer pickups automatically.

Zoom to Silver Leaf or another neighborhood (level 16+) and click Find Missing Houses. An internet connection is needed.
